How it works

Scan, read, check.

  1. 01

    Scan the barcode

    Or type it in. FoodGauge shows the ingredients and nutrition information it can find, and where each fact came from. Barcode lookups need a connection.

  2. 02

    No record? Read the label

    Photograph the ingredient list or pick an image. The text is read on your phone, and you can correct it before it is used.

  3. 03

    Check what matters to you

    Allergens you flag in Settings are highlighted. Additives are matched by E code or by name. Contains and may-contain declarations are listed separately.

Evidence first

No score without evidence.

Most products open with an At a glance card instead of an overall health score. When the information needed for a score is missing, FoodGauge withholds the score and shows what is missing or conflicting.

At a glance
Your allergens, the number of additives, and sugar, saturated fat and salt amounts.
Contains and may contain
Declared allergens and precautionary warnings are kept apart, so a trace warning is not confused with an ingredient.
Halal, vegan and vegetarian clues
Status from community records for barcodes, plus the words found in the ingredient list. These are clues, not certifications.
E codes, offline
A local catalog with a short explanation for each code. Basic Mode works without internet.

Always check the package

Source records do not replace the current package. Check the label itself when making allergy-related decisions.

Questions

Straight answers.

What is FoodGauge?+

FoodGauge is a food label scanner for iPhone, with an Android version in testing. Scan a barcode or photograph the ingredient label to see ingredients, allergens, additives, E codes and available nutrition information, with the source of each fact.

Do I need a barcode?+

No. A barcode lookup is the quickest route, but when a product has no record, or no barcode at all, you can photograph the ingredient label or choose an image from your gallery, then review and correct the extracted text.

Does FoodGauge give a health score?+

Most products show an At a glance card without an overall health score. A score is withheld when the required evidence is unavailable, and missing or conflicting information stays visible.

Can it tell me if a product is halal or vegan?+

For barcodes it shows halal, vegan and vegetarian status from community databases, and it checks the ingredient list on your phone for related words. Both are clues, not certifications.

Does it work offline?+

Basic Mode reads labels on your phone and includes the local E-code catalog, so it works without internet. New barcode lookups need a connection.

Are my photos uploaded?+

No. Label text is read on your phone. In AI mode, only the extracted text is sent for analysis, not the photo.

Is FoodGauge medical advice?+

No. FoodGauge provides general label information, not medical advice or a personalised nutrition plan. For allergy-related decisions, always check the current package.

Do I need an account?+

No. There is no login or registration. Saved products and recent inspections are available without an account.

Which languages does the app support?+

English, Turkish, Spanish, French, Russian and Arabic.

How do I get it?+

FoodGauge is free on the App Store and contains ads. The Android version is in closed testing on Google Play.
